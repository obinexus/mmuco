**Two points the supplier should know:**

- The door supplier has not been chosen. Several candidate door frames would put the latch jamb **about 50–130 mm further toward this run**, leaving a run of about **1,770–1,850 mm**. Please answer for the run as drawn, and say how your answer changes if the run is shortened by up to 130 mm.
- The drawing currently **assumes** that the run outline is the **finished face** (end panel or worktop edge). That gives a 300 mm nib with **no construction margin**. If the outline were the carcass, an 18 mm end panel would leave 282 mm.

## Information requested

1. The **end-panel construction and thickness** at the door end of the run.
2. Which surface you set out to: **finished end-panel face, worktop edge or carcass**.
3. Whether the **worktop projects beyond the end panel** at that end, and by how much (any edge trim included).
4. The **installed distance from the wall jamb to your outermost finished face** at that end, for both dwellings.
5. Your installation tolerance on that position, and the **minimum nib you can guarantee** if we specify 300 mm minimum after installation. What setting-out dimension do you recommend to achieve it?
6. How the run length is made up, end panel included, and which unit sizes you would use at 1,900 mm and at 1,770–1,850 mm.
7. Confirmation that **the same detail applies to both dwellings** (handed).
8. A **dimensioned elevation or setting-out drawing** for both runs, and **written confirmation** of the finished-face position.
9. Any appliance or service you would place in the unit at the door end.
