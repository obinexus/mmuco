---

Dear JB Kind team,

We are considering your **single pocket door system** for a kitchen-to-utility door in each of two mirrored single-storey dwellings: two systems, one of each hand. Your website size guide lists **POCSYSSGL915**:

- door size 915 × 1981;
- "Clear Opening Size (walkthrough area)": Width (OW) 890, Height (OH) 1970;
- "Structural Opening Size of the Unit": Width (SW) 1925, Height (SH) 2075;
- Finished Wall Thickness 125.

We need **at least 850 mm clear width with the door fully open, as installed**, and our partition is currently **100 mm** finished thickness. Could you please answer the questions in Appendix B and confirm the figures in Appendix C in writing? Our situation is summarised in Appendix A.

Kind regards,
[Designer name]

## Appendix B: questions

1. **100 mm wall.** Is a version of the single system available for a **100 mm** finished wall? If so, please give its codes and sizes. If 125 mm is the only option, please confirm the stud and board build-up it assumes.
2. **Clear opening definition.** How is the **890 mm "Clear Opening Size (walkthrough area)"** for POCSYSSGL915 measured? Which handle or pull, which stop setting, and how far does the leaf protrude when fully open?
3. **Structural Opening Size of the Unit.** Is the "Structural Opening Size of the Unit" (SW 1925 × SH 2075) the stud opening to build, or should a tolerance be added? How much?
4. **Jambs and linings.** Where do the latch-side and pocket-side jambs or linings sit relative to the structural opening, and how thick are they?
5. **Installed clear width** with the door fully open, for:
   - (a) flush pulls;
   - (b) an edge pull or D-handle suitable for limited grip;
   - (c) any soft-close option;
   - (d) the timber accessory kit fitted.
6. **Handing.** Reversible, or one of each hand?
7. **Pocket end at an external wall.** Is this acceptable, and what fixing and end-stud detail do you require?
8. **Fixings and services.** What may and may not be fixed to, or run within, the pocket section?
9. **Head.** Heading and track detail, and the minimum head height above finished floor level.
10. **Operation.** Typical operating force, and recommended hardware for users with limited grip.
11. **Documents.** Please send:
    - a **dated** technical datasheet and **installation drawing** for POCSYSSGL915, and for any 100 mm version;
    - **written confirmation** of your answers to questions 2–5.
