## Questions for every candidate

1. Exact product name, model or kit code, and frame type for a **single** pocket door in a **100 mm** finished wall.
2. Your **dated** technical datasheet and **installation drawing** (revision or date shown) for that configuration.
3. Overall frame width and height, and the **stud (rough) opening** to build, including any tolerance.
4. Where the latch-side jamb or receiver, and the pocket-side jamb and lining, sit relative to the stud opening.
5. **Installed clear width** with the door fully open, for:
   - (a) flush pulls with the stop set as you recommend;
   - (b) an edge pull or D-handle;
   - (c) any fully-retracting option.
   Please state the stop setting and leaf protrusion assumed.
6. Effect of soft-close, self-close or damper options on the fully-open position and the clear width.
7. Stud size and board build-up for 100 mm and 125 mm finished walls.
8. Head detail: track, fixings and the minimum head height above finished floor.
9. Restrictions on fixings, sockets, switches, pipes and ducts on or within the pocket section.
10. Operating force, and hardware suitable for users with limited grip.
11. **Written confirmation** that items 3–6 apply to the configuration offered for this project.

## Enquiry A: ECLISSE (ECLISSE UK Ltd), Classic Single

**Basis:** datasheet `ECL-Classic-Single-UK-26`, read 24 Oct 2026, row PW 900 × PH 2,030, overall W 1,894 × H 2,115, door panel 926 × 2,040.

- Is this the current issue of the datasheet for the PW 900 × 2,030 frame in a **100 mm** finished wall?
- The datasheet gives **900 as the maximum passage (PW)** and recommends setting the stop so the door protrudes with flush pulls. What is the **installed clear width** with that stop setting? With the PUSH&PULL accessory?
- What do the **22 mm** and **5 mm** dimensions at the ends of W mean on the elevation? Does the stud opening equal W + 3–5 mm, or W + 22 + 5 + tolerance?
- What do the **25 mm** dimensions either side of PW represent: jamb linings?
- For comparison: the PW 850 × 2,020 frame (W 1,844). What is its installed clear width with flush pulls?

## Enquiry B: JB Kind, single pocket door system

**Basis:** product page size guide, read 24 Oct 2026, code POCSYSSGL915: door 915 × 1,981, "Clear Opening Size (walkthrough area)" 890 × 1,970, structural opening 1,925 × 2,075, finished wall 125.

- Is a **100 mm** finished-wall version available? If so, what are its sizes?
- How is the 890 mm "walkthrough" clear opening measured? With which pull, and with the stop where?
- Is the 1,925 mm structural opening the stud opening to build, or is a tolerance to be added?
