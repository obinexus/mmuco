**Subject:** Technical enquiry: single pocket frame for at least 850 mm clear in a 100 mm plasterboard partition (UK project, 2 no., handed)

---

Dear Scrigno technical team,

We are considering a Scrigno single pocket door frame for a kitchen-to-utility door in each of two mirrored single-storey dwellings in the UK: two frames, one of each hand.

Your FAQ (30 August 2024) states that Scrigno Base plasterboard counter frames are available for **100 mm and 125 mm** finished walls, on 75 and 100 mm profiles respectively. A UK retailer lists two versions of a Scrigno "926 × 2040" single system: a standard one and a fire-rated one. Their figures differ, and neither listing names the Scrigno product line, so we would like your official data.

We need **at least 850 mm clear width with the door fully open, as installed**, in a **100 mm** finished plasterboard partition. Could you please answer the questions in Appendix B and confirm the figures in Appendix C in writing? Our situation is summarised in Appendix A.

Kind regards,
[Designer name]

## Appendix B: questions

1. **Product.** Which Scrigno product line and code suits a single door giving **≥ 850 mm installed clear width** in a **100 mm** finished plasterboard wall? Is it available for UK supply?
2. **Official dimensions** for that frame: overall frame width and height, the stud opening to build (and any tolerance), the passage / clear opening, door leaf size and thickness, finished wall thickness and internal frame thickness.
3. **Retailer figures.** Two listings by one UK retailer for a "926 × 2040" single system state the following. We have kept them separate, because they appear to be different products:
   - (a) structural opening 1,910 × 2,140, walk-through 900 × 2,030, cassette width 900, finished wall 125;
   - (b) for a fire-rated version: structural opening 1,930 × 2,005–2,505, walk-through 896 × 1,900–2,400, with the clear opening "reduced in width by 10mm", finished wall 125.

   Which Scrigno product line and code does **each** listing correspond to, and what are the official dimensions of each? Is the 896 mm figure already net of the 10 mm reduction, and what does that reduction represent?
4. **Installed clear width** with the door fully open, for:
   - (a) flush pulls with your recommended stop setting (please state the leaf protrusion);
   - (b) an edge pull suitable for limited grip;
   - (c) soft-close or self-closing devices. A retailer states that a self-closing mechanism reduces the clear opening by 50 mm: please confirm.
5. **Jambs and linings.** Their positions relative to the stud opening, and their thickness.
6. **Handing.** Reversible, or one of each hand?
7. **Pocket end at an external wall.** Is this acceptable, and what fixing and end-stud detail do you require?
8. **Fixings and services.** What may and may not be fixed to, or run within, the pocket section?
9. **Head.** Track and heading detail, and the minimum head height above finished floor level.
10. **Operation.** Typical operating force, and recommended hardware for users with limited grip.
11. **Documents.** Please send:
    - the **dated** technical datasheet and **installation drawing** for the recommended frame;
    - **written confirmation** of your answers to questions 2–5.
