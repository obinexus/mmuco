## Appendix B: questions

1. **Current issue and code.** Is `ECL-Classic-Single-UK-26` the current specification for this frame? Please give the ordering code for a single CLASSIC frame PW 900 × PH 2030 (door panel 926 × 2040) for a **100 mm** finished wall, and confirm that it is a different code from the PW 900 × PH 1971 frame (door 914 × 1981). Your Technical page lists CLASSIC assembly instructions for **70/90** and **75/100** profiles: which applies to a 100 mm finished wall?
2. **Stud opening.** Is the stud opening to build **W 1,894 + 3–5 mm** wide and **H 2,115 + 3–5 mm** high, as the datasheet note suggests?
   - What do the **22 mm** and **5 mm** dimensions at the ends of W on the elevation represent?
   - What does the **12.5 mm** on the plan represent?
   - Do any of these add to the stud opening?
3. **Jambs and linings.** What do the **25 mm** dimensions either side of PW on the plan represent? Where do the latch-side and pocket-side jamb linings sit relative to the stud opening?
4. **Installed clear width** with the door fully open, for each of:
   - (a) flush pulls, with the stop regulated as your notes recommend; please state the leaf protrusion;
   - (b) the **PUSH&PULL** accessory;
   - (c) **BIAS** or **BIAS DS** soft close;
   - (d) the self-closing mechanism;
   - (e) an edge pull suitable for users with limited grip.
5. **Clear passage height** with the timber lining kit fitted.
6. **Door leaf.** Please confirm the leaf size to order for this frame (926 × 2040, 35–44 mm thick?). Do you supply or recommend a leaf?
7. **Handing.** Is the frame reversible, or must we order one of each hand?
8. **Pocket end at an external wall.** Is it acceptable for the pocket end of the frame to abut the inner face of an external wall? What fixing and end-stud detail do you require?
9. **Fixings and services.** What may and may not be fixed to, or run within, the pocket section? Is your CLASSIC **WIRING-READY** frame available in this size, should a switch be needed within the frame zone?
10. **Head.** Track and heading detail, and the minimum head height above finished floor level.
11. **Operation.** Typical operating force, with and without soft close, and your recommended hardware for users with limited grip.
12. **Comparison only.** The same answers to questions 2–4 for **PW 850 × 2020 (W 1,844 × H 2,105)**, in particular its installed clear width with flush pulls.
13. **Documents.** Please send:
    - the dated specification (or confirm UK-26);
    - the installation / assembly drawing for the relevant profile;
    - CAD or BIM files if available;
    - **written confirmation** of your answers to questions 2–4 for this configuration.
