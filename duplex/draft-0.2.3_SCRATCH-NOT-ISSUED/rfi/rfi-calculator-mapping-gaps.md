# RFI → calculator mapping: gaps, proposed fixes and dispositions (Audit B)

**Status (revision 6, 24 Oct 2026):**

- The adopted calculator, `evidence/scripts/gate_calc.mjs` (SHA-256 `f4f0ddcb…`), and its template are **unchanged**.
- Fixes for gaps G1–G10 are implemented **only as a proposal** in `CALCULATOR_PROPOSAL_NOT_ADOPTED/`, with tests. They await review and authorisation.
- Every RFI item now has an explicit disposition against that proposal: `RFI_CALCULATOR_FIELD_DISPOSITIONS.csv`.

**Basis:**

- The traceability files are generated by `evidence/scripts/rfi_trace.mjs`. It parses each RFI's numbered questions, sub-items, follow-on paragraphs and Appendix C rows, and it fails if any item has no mapping or disposition.
- **The item count is now 91, not 89.** Finding F-C1 / F-C2 split RFI_01C Appendix C into separate standard and fire-rated rows, so it has 10 rows instead of 8.
- Generator result: no item without a mapping, no item without a disposition, no unknown field.

## 2. Dispositions against the PROPOSED calculator

`RFI_CALCULATOR_FIELD_DISPOSITIONS.csv` gives each of the 91 items exactly one disposition:

| Disposition | Items | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| VALIDATED_FIELD, influences a calculation | 31 | Every listed field is CALC: changing it changes a calculated output |
| VALIDATED_FIELD, influences a calculation, with echo-only context | 27 | At least one CALC field, plus echo-only fields (configuration, basis, passage height) |
| VALIDATED_FIELD, echo only | 24 | Validated text or number, copied to the output; no calculation uses it (`doorSystem.*`, `kit.passageHeight_mm`) |
| EVIDENCE_ONLY | 9 | No calculator input is needed. Recorded as the manufacturer's written answer in `POCKET_DOOR_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv` and cited in `doorSystem.manufacturerWrittenConfirmationRef` |
| SCHEMA_GAP | **0** | No item is left without a field or an evidence record |
| **Total** | **91** | |

**How "influences a calculation" was established:** by test, not by declaration.

- `tests/run_tests.mjs` changes each of the 59 fields on its own and compares every output.
- **31 fields are CALC, 24 are ECHO and 4 are NONE (template help).** All agree with the roles declared in the code.
- The CSV column `field_roles_tested` shows the role of every field listed for an item.

**The 9 EVIDENCE_ONLY items:**

- 01A: Q2.2 (meaning of 12.5), Q9 (fixings, services, WIRING-READY), Q11 (operating force), Q13.3 (CAD / BIM), C10 (pocket-side lining).
- 01B: Q8 (fixings), Q10 (operating force).
- 01C: Q8 (fixings), Q10 (operating force).

**The pocket-side lining is evidence only** because it does not change the kit geometry. Its effect on clear width enters through the installed width or reduction.

**Agreement check (run by the generator):**

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Every item has a disposition | Yes (91 of 91) |
| Every field named in a disposition exists in the code (`FIELDS`) | Yes |
| Every field named exists in the proposed template | Yes |

| Test suite | 64 of 64 passed |
| **Verdict** | **Complete against the proposed calculator (not adopted): the code, schema and tests agree for every item. Not complete against the adopted calculator** |

**Conditions attached to that verdict:**

- **16 Appendix C cells** in the RFIs ("Our calculator field") name adopted fields that the proposal replaces or adds to. They are listed in the column `rfi_column_update_on_adoption`. They were deliberately not changed, so that the RFIs do not cite an unadopted schema. Update them only on adoption.
- **Some field choices depend on the manufacturer's answer.** The disposition states the rule:
  - JB Kind's "Structural Opening Size of the Unit" (01B C1, Q3), and Scrigno's structural openings (01C C2, C3): `kit.studOpeningWidth_mm` if it is the opening to build, otherwise `kit.overallFrameWidth_mm` + tolerance.
  - Scrigno's 896 (01C C6, Q3b): the installed width if it is already net of the 10 mm, otherwise a 10 mm reduction.
- **Validation cannot detect a figure entered under the wrong definition.** That remains the designer's responsibility.

## 3. Gaps G1–G10 and the proposed fixes (implemented in the proposal, NOT adopted)

- All four existing input files (11 scenarios) give **identical** results in the proposal for every value the adopted calculator outputs.
